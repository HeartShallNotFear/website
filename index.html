Mr Now

SIQA AIMA nominated artist Mr Now puts the flex and the fallout on the same record. “Reckless” earned his Best AI Hip Hop Song nomination. “Shining in the Dark” gets personal: sleepless nights, feeling unheard, barely keeping afloat, and waking up wanting more. Elsewhere, he’s counting money, checking on someone whose smile has changed, or wondering whether he should finally clean the crib. His debut album, Remastered (Reinvented), arrives through Heart Shall Not Fear with all of those versions of him intact.

Remastered (Reinvented)

Album · 2026 · Heart Shall Not Fear

Mr Now’s eleven-song album opens with “Reckless” and closes with “Shining in the Dark.” Hip hop leads the record, with “What’s Wrong?” moving into soul funk and “Still Dreaming” into dance.

Love Remains

CrimineyJay

Album · Released July 31, 2026

The album begins by naming different forms of love. Across nine songs, those lines stop holding. Brotherhood meets accountability. Boundaries become an act of love. The final song asks whether love changed or the person living through it did.

Available now on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music and YouTube Music.
