Mr Now

SIQA AIMA nominated artist Mr Now puts the flex and the fallout on the same record. “Reckless” earned his Best AI Hip Hop Song nomination. “Shining in the Dark” is a late-night song about feeling unheard, barely keeping afloat and waking up wanting more. Elsewhere, he’s counting money, checking on someone whose smile has changed or wondering whether he should finally clean the crib. On his debut album, Remastered (Reinvented), he leaves all of those sides in.

Remastered (Reinvented)

Album · 2026 · Heart Shall Not Fear

“Reckless” opens the eleven-song album and “Shining in the Dark” closes it. Hip hop leads. “What’s Wrong?” turns toward soul funk; “Still Dreaming” goes into dance.

Artist

CrimineyJay

From Fontana by way of Atlanta, CrimineyJay is building Soul Concrete from R&B and soul, with room for the songs to wander into other genres. Love Remains starts with Agape, Phileo and Eros, then works its way through brotherhood, accountability and boundaries. He’s making the records he wishes he could find.

About the label

Heart Shall Not Fear
